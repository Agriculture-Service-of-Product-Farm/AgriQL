A language, not a forecast

AgriQL cannot predict yield.

AgriQL is a database language. It can hold rain, moisture, and fertiliser as separate measurements. It cannot grow a plant, and it cannot tell you what the harvest will be. The drawing only shows why those three figures are not added.

Sown · illustration

  1. 25 mmrain
  2. 25 %moisture
  3. 25 kgfertiliser

Refused. Millimetres, percent, and kilograms are not one quantity.

Moving the control changes the illustration. It does not calculate a yield. Millimetres may be added to metres. They may not be added to kilograms.
