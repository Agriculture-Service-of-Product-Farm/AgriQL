Engineering a record of growth

The plant moves. The measurements do not collapse.

A season of study is a sequence of observations. Rain, soil moisture, and fertiliser can be written beside the same plant. They are not one number, and they are not added.

  1. 25 mmrain, through the season
  2. 25 %soil moisture, at a reading
  3. 25 kgfertiliser, applied once

25 + 25 + 25 refused

AgriQL does not grow the plant and does not predict a yield. It keeps the study from treating three quantities as one. Millimetres may be added to metres. Millimetres may not be added to kilograms.

What the drawing is

Movement, then a record.

The stem is drawn once, from the soil upward. The line that travels with it is a mark of the drawing, not a sensor. The three lines beside it are the same example used elsewhere on this site: an explanation, not a field result.

What a study still needs

One file, checked.

The observations of a season belong in one .aqdb file. A profile can show empty values. A time column can be checked for gaps. A gap is not a reading of zero. This release still reads one table at a time, and it still does not join tables.
