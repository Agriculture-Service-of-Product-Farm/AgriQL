Move the season. The quantities stay apart.

The plant below is drawn. It is not growing in a field, and the numbers are not a reading. Rain, moisture, and fertiliser can stand beside the same study. They are not added.

Refused. Millimetres, percent, and kilograms are not one quantity.

AgriQL does not grow the plant and does not predict a yield. Millimetres may be added to metres. They may not be added to kilograms.
